Welcome to Statistics in the Modern World

1Statistics in the Modern World¶

An interactive companion for MATH 1209 at CSU Bakersfield.

Statistics is the science of learning from data — and data is everywhere: in the news you read, the ads you scroll past, the health advice you get, and the decisions you make about money, work, and school. This book will help you become someone who can look at a number, a graph, or a headline and ask the right question: How do they know that? Should I believe it?

We keep the examples close to everyday life on purpose. You will meet a shelter full of cats and dogs, a coffee shop timing its morning rush, students comparing their sleep and screen time, and a phone that keeps dying at the worst moment. The statistics is the new part — the stories are ones you already understand.

1.1How this book works¶

Every topic is built in the same friendly order, so you always know what to expect:

The rhythm of each section

  1. The idea, in plain words — what we are trying to figure out and why it matters.

  2. The picture — a graph or a diagram, because statistics is easier to see.

  3. The method — the formula or the steps, with every symbol explained the first time it appears.

  4. A fully worked example — start to finish, before we ask you to try one.

  5. On your calculator — the TI-83/84 button sequence.

  6. See it in R — the same result using the free mosaic/BSDA tools, so you can check your work and build a skill employers value.

You can succeed in this course with just a calculator. The R boxes are there to show you the method working and to give you a head start on a tool used across many careers — they are always optional unless your instructor says otherwise.

1.2What you’ll be able to do by December¶

  • Tell a good study from a misleading one, and know when “causes” is fair to say.

  • Summarize data with the right graph and the right numbers.

  • Reason about chance and the Normal (“bell curve”) model.

  • Build and interpret a confidence interval and run a hypothesis test — the two ideas at the heart of how researchers make claims from data.

Accessibility

This book is built to WCAG 2.1 AA. Every figure has a text description, colors are colorblind-safe and readable in grayscale, and the whole book is available as tagged PDF and screen-reader-ready HTML. Need another format? Ask your instructor — it already exists.
