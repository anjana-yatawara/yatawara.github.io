# Answers to Odd Practice Items

Below are brief final answers to the **odd-numbered** "Check your understanding" items
at the end of each weekly unit, so you can self-check your work. Only the final answer
is given — a number, a word, or a one-line conclusion — not the full worked steps. If
your answer does not match, redo the matching worked example in that week before
moving on. (Weeks 7 and 12 are review-and-exam weeks; their practice lives in the
worked exam-style examples inside those units, so they have no items here.)

## Week 11 — Hypothesis Testing Logic

1. $H_0: p = 0.90$ versus $H_A: p < 0.90$; a one-sided (left-tailed) test.
3. The classmate wrongly reads the p-value as $P(H_0 \text{ true})$; correctly, $p \approx 0.0033$ is the chance of a sample this extreme *if $H_0$ were true*.
5. At $\alpha = 0.05$, reject $H_0$; at $\alpha = 0.01$, fail to reject — the decision depends on the significance level chosen before seeing the data.
