## Standard Normal Distribution Table

The table below gives $P(Z \leq z) = \Phi(z)$ for the standard normal distribution $Z \sim N(0, 1)$.

## Common Distribution Summary

| Distribution | PMF/PDF | E(X) | V(X) | MGF |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|
| Binomial$(n, p)$ | $\binom{n}{x}p^x(1-p)^{n-x}$ | $np$ | $np(1-p)$ | $(pe^t + 1-p)^n$ |
| Geometric$(p)$ | $p(1-p)^{x-1}$ | $1/p$ | $(1-p)/p^2$ | $pe^t/[1-(1-p)e^t]$ |
| Poisson$(\lambda)$ | $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^x/x!$ | $\lambda$ | $\lambda$ | $e^{\lambda(e^t - 1)}$ |
| Uniform$(a,b)$ | $1/(b-a)$ | $(a+b)/2$ | $(b-a)^2/12$ | $(e^{tb}-e^{ta})/[t(b-a)]$ |
| Normal$(\mu, \sigma^2)$ | $\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}$ | $\mu$ | $\sigma^2$ | $e^{\mu t + \sigma^2 t^2/2}$ |
| Exponential$(\beta)$ | $\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-x/\beta}$ | $\beta$ | $\beta^2$ | $(1-\beta t)^{-1}$ |
| Gamma$(\alpha, \beta)$ | $\frac{x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}}{\beta^\alpha\Gamma(\alpha)}$ | $\alpha\beta$ | $\alpha\beta^2$ | $(1-\beta t)^{-\alpha}$ |
| Beta$(\alpha, \beta)$ | $\frac{x^{\alpha-1}(1-x)^{\beta-1}}{B(\alpha,\beta)}$ | $\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}$ | $\frac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)}$ | *complex* |
