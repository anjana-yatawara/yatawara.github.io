Powers of Ten

A game about scale · 26 orders of magnitude

You are a single mote of dust, twenty-two micrometres across, and the camera is all the way in. Eat anything smaller, flee anything larger, and fill the bar.

When it fills, the world zooms out by an order of magnitude and you become the next thing up the ladder. Everything you spent the last ten seconds learning collapses to a speck — and it leaves a labelled ring behind, so by the end the field is littered with the worlds you outgrew. Each rung changes the physics under you, not just the picture.

Play Powers of Ten

Plays on a phone or a keyboard. No install, no account.

Built for a course

Acts of the Continuous

MATH 3200 · Exam 2

A revision game in six acts, following the second-exam material of MATH 3200: continuous densities, the normal family, the gamma family and its relatives, moment generating functions, and joint distributions.

Each question carries a written explanation behind the answer, so a wrong choice is still worth reading. The acts unlock in order and keep a best score of their own, and a name entered at the end appears on a shared leaderboard.

Open the game

The six acts

  1. Act I

    Foundations

    Densities and CDFs, expectation, variance, linear transformations.

  2. Act II

    The Bell

    The normal distribution: standardise, look up, compare.

  3. Act III

    The Gamma Dynasty

    One family covering the exponential, chi-squared and Erlang.

  4. Act IV

    Exp & Beta

    Waiting times, chi-squared as a gamma in disguise, and the beta on the unit interval.

  5. Act V

    The MGF Toolkit

    Moment generating functions: sums of independent variables, linear transforms.

  6. Act VI

    Two-Variable World

    Joint densities, marginals, independence, covariance, conditionals.

Arcade games

Orbital

One button · space bar or tap

You are locked in orbit; let go and you fly dead straight until the next planet’s gravity catches you. Release late and tight for a perfect and a rising multiplier, while a void climbs from below and never stops gaining.

Open the game

Revenge of the Basco

One input · space bar or tap

A single-button run: press to stay airborne and thread the gaps between columns of fire. The longer the run lasts the less the columns hold still, and there are bosses waiting further in.

Open the game

Sigma Strike

Wave shooter · arrow keys and space, or touch

A statistics-themed space shooter. Clear each wave, chain hits for a combo multiplier, pick up weapon, shield and bomb upgrades, and meet a boss on every tenth wave.
